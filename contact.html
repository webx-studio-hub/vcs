FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Call us on +91 96142 00074 or drop by the office.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes, your first assessment is free. We’ll understand your goal and tell you which visa route suits you.

Which countries do you help with?

We handle visas for the UK, Canada, Australia, the USA and Europe.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Your passport (if you have one), your latest educational certificates and marksheets, and any English test results. We’ll give you a full checklist for your specific visa.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 6:30 PM. You can also call or WhatsApp us.

Do you also book air tickets and hotels?

Yes. Along with visas, we help with passport applications, air tickets and hotel bookings.

I failed Class 9 or +1. Can you help?

Yes, through Dakha Academy. See the Dakha Academy page or call +91 98031 92031.
