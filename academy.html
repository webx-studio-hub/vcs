Questions

Parents & students often ask

Can’t find your answer? Call Dakha Academy on +91 98031 92031.

I failed Class 9. Do I have to repeat it?

Not necessarily. Through open schooling you can appear directly for Class 10 instead of repeating Class 9. Call us to check the current admission dates.

I failed +1 (Class 11). Can I still do +2?

Yes. Dakha Academy helps +1-fail students go directly to +2 through NIOS or Punjab Open School.

Is it only for PSEB students?

No. Students from both PSEB and CBSE are welcome.

Which board should I choose, NIOS or Punjab Open School?

It depends on your situation. We explain both options and help you choose the one that suits you best.

Can I plan a study visa after completing 10th or +2?

Once you’ve completed your qualification, our VCS team can guide you on study options abroad and what each country requires.
