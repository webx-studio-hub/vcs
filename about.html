Who we are

Honest advice, from the first question to the day you fly

VCS Immigration Consultants (V Consultants & Services) helps students, families and travellers with study visas, visitor visas, sponsor visas and spouse work permits for the UK, Canada, Australia, the USA and Europe, plus passports, air tickets and hotel bookings.

Under the same roof, Dakha Academy helps students who failed Class 9 or +1 complete their 10th and +2 through NIOS and Punjab Open School, so a lost year never stands between them and their future.
